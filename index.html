MATERIAL + DEPTH

Material v2 writes albedo, pseudo-Z, XYZ normals, roughness, metalness, local material AO and emissive into a WebGL2 MRT G-buffer. Legacy bump/spec remains only for fallback/A-B comparison.

MATERIAL DEBUG

PLAYER LIGHT

The player omni and mouse cone are occluded by the same terrain rectangles used by robot line-of-sight. The omni intensity is independent of the global emissive multiplier so the player remains faintly readable in near-black scenes.

LIGHT GROUPS

Per-group controls feed the bounded 16-light shader budget. Global Emissive strength still scales world/object lights; the player omni and cone have their own direct intensity controls.

SURFACE FX

SurfaceFX remains responsible for water and optional low-level GrassField texture/detail. Physical readable plants are owned by FoliageFX below.

FOLIAGE FX

Root-locked, opaque HD atlas foliage. Quality 0 keeps the plants static; 1–3 progressively add coherent wind, pseudo-normal shading, bounded actor interaction and tip flutter.

FX

MACHINE FRAGMENT ORBIT

Values are editable directly. Ambient light fades from INITIAL to COMPLETE after the room's final objective. Press B during play to force the complete-light state globally.
