## Established clipping mechanism and incomplete lighting qualification

**Measured result:** final source `9b5b932` has 120,753 object-owned pixels;
99.8575605% are at or below one display code in every RGB channel after post.
Raw lighting is nonzero. All 120,581 black occupied pixels in the final frame
are exactly zero, not just below the one-code threshold. The original stage report uses object ID != 0 as mask
authority. The earlier `8285c61` report's convenience mask used non-black albedo;
its separate analysis recomputes authoritative object-ID occupancy explicitly.

**Established root cause:** the inherited shadow lift `-0.10` subtracts directly
from linear HDR. Exponential exposure is followed by contrast `1.12`, which adds
a negative black offset, and negative results clamp before the final IEC sRGB
transfer. With neutral grade and the recorded defaults, the neutral cutoff is
approximately **0.12650575 linear**. The final display transfer exists and cannot
recover already-clipped values.
